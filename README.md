# Equity Research & Fundamental Analysis – Indian Banking

This repository contains academic equity research and fundamental analysis focused on the Indian banking space.
The work emphasizes understanding profitability drivers, asset quality, operating efficiency, and valuation logic in large private-sector banks.

## Scope of Analysis
The analysis primarily covers HDFC Bank and ICICI Bank as representative leaders of India’s private banking system.
These institutions are used to study sector-level themes such as deposit franchise strength, ROE sustainability, margin dynamics, and capital efficiency.

## Projects Included

### ICICI Bank – Equity Research Pitch Deck
- Sell-side style equity research deck covering sector context, business model, financial performance, peer comparison, valuation rationale and key risks.
- Focuses on ROE-led valuation logic and balance-sheet quality rather than short-term price prediction.

### HDFC Bank – One-Page Fundamental Profile
- Concise, institutional-style one-page snapshot analyzing multi-year trends in profitability, efficiency, asset quality and valuation metrics.
- Designed for quick fundamental assessment rather than investment recommendation.
